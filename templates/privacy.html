ParkingBuzz

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ParkingBuzz ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. ParkingBuzz is designed as a vehicle safety contact tool that enables bystanders or other drivers to contact vehicle owners without exposing either party's personal phone number or private identity.

2. Information We Collect

  • Vehicle & Tag Identifiers: Anonymous vehicle tag IDs and optional vehicle nickname used to route parking alerts.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens required to deliver real-time notifications to your device when your tag is scanned.
  • Communications Data: Temporary alert payloads (e.g. "Car blocked", "Headlights on") transmitted during active user sessions.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect or sell personal phone numbers to third parties.
  • We do NOT track real-time GPS locations.
  • We do NOT display advertising or use third-party advertising tracking IDs.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is strictly utilized to:

  • Route instant push notifications and parking alerts to the vehicle owner.
  • Facilitate private, masked audio/chat sessions between a contactor and owner.
  • Maintain system security and prevent spam or abuse.

5. Data Security & Storage

All communication between the mobile application, web interface, and backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. Data is stored on secured cloud servers with strict access controls.

6. Account & Data Deletion

Users have the full right to delete their registered tags and associated data at any time. To request tag removal or data deletion, contact us at support@parkingbuzz.alert or utilize the tag deactivation options in your dashboard. Upon confirmation, all associated tokens and records are permanently removed within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

ParkingBuzz is intended for vehicle owners and licensed drivers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
